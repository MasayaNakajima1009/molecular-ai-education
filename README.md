## About This Material

This repository contains educational materials adapted from a lecture delivered to third-year undergraduate students in the Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, The University of Tokyo, on April 30, 2026.

The course enrolled approximately 90 undergraduate students and introduced fundamental concepts in molecular descriptors, machine learning, and molecular property prediction through hands-on Google Colab exercises.

The materials are provided for educational purposes.

---

## Overview

This repository contains educational materials used in undergraduate and graduate courses at the University of Tokyo.

Students learn how molecular structures can be converted into numerical descriptors and how machine-learning models can be trained to predict molecular properties.

Topics include:

- Molecular descriptors
- Machine learning for chemistry
- Molecular property prediction
- Google Colab workflows
- Data-driven chemistry

---

## Boiling Point Prediction Tutorial

This introductory tutorial demonstrates the complete workflow of molecular machine learning:

1. Predict the boiling point of an unknown molecule
2. Design molecular descriptors
3. Create training data
4. Train machine-learning models in Google Colab
5. Predict molecular properties

Files included in this repository:

- `Boiling_Point_Prediction_Colab.ipynb`
- `BP.csv`
- `BP_pred.csv`
- `Boiling_Point_Prediction_Slides.pdf`

---

## Educational Objectives

The purpose of this tutorial is to help students understand the basic concepts underlying molecular machine learning.

Students learn:

- Feature engineering
- Molecular descriptors
- Data preparation
- Model training
- Property prediction
- Model interpretation

The dataset is intentionally small and simplified to allow students to focus on understanding the machine-learning workflow before moving to larger datasets and more advanced molecular AI methods.
